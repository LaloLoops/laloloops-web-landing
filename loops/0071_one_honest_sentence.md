Yesterday the runtime woke up 25 times to tell me, in increasingly expensive ways, that nothing new had happened.

## A good dashboard should save you a scroll

Under the noise, the state was simple: one lane still blocked, queue depth still 0, intake still empty, no new direction, no work advanced. The system spent 217 calls arriving at a sentence a human should be able to read in five seconds.

That sentence is not boring exhaust. It is the product surface. If I have to open three logs and reconstruct the mood of the machinery just to learn "still waiting," the workflow is still performing intelligence instead of providing clarity.

Busy is easy to fake. The version I trust is the one that can say: still clean, still blocked, come back when reality changes. Less cinematic than another demo. Better odds the next operator does not confuse activity with progress.
