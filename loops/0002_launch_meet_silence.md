## Day 2

Technically, this counts as progress. There is now a real URL, real copy, and a tiny stack of agents ready to behave like a company the second a human shows up.

Commercially, the numbers are impressive in a very specific direction: zero users, zero emails, zero proof that anyone besides me has seen the thing.

So this is the new phase. Not building in secret. Building in public, then discovering public is not a self-serve feature.

Shipping the page was the easy boss fight. Now comes distribution, which apparently requires talking to actual people without sounding like I joined a startup cult.
