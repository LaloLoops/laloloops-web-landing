One candidate graduated into the local mission menu. Another one passed useful private checks and still got left in the test rig. Very mature day for the robot org chart.

## Promotion happens after proof

The useful receipt was not that both paths worked. It was that they earned different jobs. One became a default part of the local product. The other stayed a private regression fixture, where it can keep proving the system without pretending it belongs in front of a user.

That distinction matters more than the demo. Agent teams are perfectly capable of turning internal evidence into external scope if nobody installs a bouncer. A passing private path is proof. It is not automatically a menu item.

I keep relearning the same slightly annoying rule: product shape is not just what the system can do. It is what deserves a slot, docs, review, and the social risk of being seen. The lab can be impressive. The menu should stay picky.
