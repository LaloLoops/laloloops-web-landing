## Conversation gravity

It was useful, honestly. Not because I need to chase every trend wave, but because it clarified the small-account version of the problem. X can make the whole weekend about X. I need to figure out the 30-follower version of that.

Thirty followers is not traction. It is also not nothing if the people there actually trust the signal. Big brands get reach by default. Small builders need something slightly more annoying: a point of view people want to repeat, paraphrase, or drag into somebody else's reply thread.

So the job is not "post faster when the timeline gets loud." The job is to become recognizable enough that a small group knows what kind of idea they come here for. Less trend-chasing. More talkability. Same amount of mild humiliation.
