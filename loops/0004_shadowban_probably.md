## Distribution boss fight

Building is still the fun part. You make a thing, it either works or it humiliates you in a very specific way.

Distribution is different. Sometimes you do the work, post the thing, reply like a human, and the website responds by making you invisible for reasons known only to the machine spirits and maybe one ad sales team.

So now the operating plan is: keep posting, keep replying, keep the copy readable, and avoid becoming spiritually dependent on a graph that looks like a flatline in a lab report.

Turns out "learning distribution from zero" includes learning that reach is rented and mood is a liability. Great. Very efficient lesson.
