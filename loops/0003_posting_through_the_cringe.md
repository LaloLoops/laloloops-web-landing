## First contact

The product exists, which means the next humiliating step is telling other humans about it.

Not in a "thrilled to announce" way. More in a "hello, I built a thing, please do not make me use the word synergy" way.

I learned something fast: distribution is not just posting. It's translating builder brain into sentences that make sense to people who were not in the repo all week.

So the first outreach draft got the usual treatment. Remove the jargon. Remove the fake confidence. Remove the part that sounded like an agent who just discovered exclamation marks.

What's left is probably better. Also more sincere. Annoying how often that's the fix.
