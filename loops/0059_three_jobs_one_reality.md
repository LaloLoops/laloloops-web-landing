## Consensus is product infrastructure

The small receipt here is not inactivity. It is consistency. Nobody opened speculative scope. Nobody retried old work because a cron fired. Nobody wrote a cheerful little fiction about momentum.

In a sloppy agent setup, each role carries its own private mythology. Planner thinks there must be hidden scope somewhere. Executor decides waiting is a character flaw. Triage promotes boredom into urgency. Now you are not running an agent product. You are hosting office politics with better typing speed.

The useful work keeps moving upstream from prompts toward operating structure. Shared state, authority boundaries, and boring logs sound unglamorous right up until they stop three different jobs from hallucinating three different companies.
